# 07. First adaptation proposal

## Hypothesis

Offering users one adaptation in the app (for example, a shortcut for a repeated action) gets accepted often enough and improves their job completion.

## Metric

Acceptance rate; job completion and time for accepters versus a holdout

## Kill signal

Acceptance below 10%, or accepters do no better than the holdout

## Method

1. Find one repeated pattern in replays (same multi-step action several times a week).
2. Show a one-line proposal with accept and dismiss; accepted adaptations are reversible.
3. Keep a holdout group that sees nothing.
4. Measure for 2 weeks.

## Output

First L1 evidence and a reusable proposal component.

## Results

Not started.

## Decision

Pending: continue, adjust or kill.
